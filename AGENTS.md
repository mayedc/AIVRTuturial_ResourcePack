# Agent notes

This repository is a teaching pack. Students modify prepared assets.

- Do not recreate the car, robot, city, roads, or jet meshes.
- Do not overwrite `Assets/AI_VR_Workshop_V4/Scenes/Workshop_Desktop.unity` or `Workshop_MetaVR.unity`. Save a checkpoint scene first.
- Do not delete or retarget `WorkshopDrive`, `WorkshopSpeedControl`, `WorkshopRobotWalk`, or `DriverEyeAnchor` on the teacher scenes.
- Do not vendor Unity, Meta, or MCP packages into `Assets`.
- Do not commit `Library`, `Logs`, `UserSettings`, generated project files, or `.cursor/mcp.json`.
- Do not claim a Quest 3 pass unless a headset test was actually run.
- Keep new public text in English. Preserve third-party files.
