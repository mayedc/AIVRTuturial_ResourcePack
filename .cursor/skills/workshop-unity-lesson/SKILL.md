Read [the Unity lesson](../../../Docs/Lesson02_Unity.md) and [the route behavior and desktop interaction specification](../../../Docs/03_Unity_Integration.md). Confirm the project, scene and Console through MCP.

- Save a checkpoint before editing. Create and retain one AsterGT root, correctly assigned scripts, two non-bridging road colliders, robot and DriverEyeAnchor.
- In Prompt 1.3, build the entire scene from the approved student FBX and three source FBXs through the new UnitySource Editor builder in a blank URP project. Verify one StudentVisual, materials and the fixed-speed route to the gap.
- In Prompt 1.4 (still Lesson 2), implement speed and flight **only after complete 1.3 Play** in the student's own runtime scripts. Work from the starter and exercise requirements. Keep the braking and yield intact.
- Compile, run the active-scene validator, and observe the route in Play. Record the outcome in the current checkpoint. Record headset checks separately.
